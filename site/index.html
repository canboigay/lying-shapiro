Who is Brian Shapiro?

Brian Shapiro is a Las Vegas talk-radio host. He hosts Pushing The Limits on KSHP 1400 AM and 107.1 FM, weekdays 12pm to 2pm Pacific, and streams the show live on YouTube, Facebook and X. He has been on Las Vegas radio for over 20 years and is known for confrontational debates with callers and guests.

Is Brian Shapiro related to Ben Shapiro?

No. Brian Shapiro of Pushing The Limits (Las Vegas) is not Ben Shapiro of The Daily Wire, and they are not related. The shared surname causes constant confusion, which this archive exists partly to document and partly to enjoy.

What does "MAGA mushroom gobbler" mean?

It is Brian Shapiro's insult for a blindly loyal Trump supporter. He made it his signature chant on Pushing The Limits, repeating it constantly, alongside "MAGA is dead", usually aimed at callers and at streamer Jimmy Dore's audience.

What is a "polite dodge"?

Brian Shapiro's signature debate move: a warm, courteous opener ("I'm glad you asked", "That's a fair question") followed by a pivot away from the question that was asked. This archive has logged 178 timestamped polite dodges.

What is Lying Shapiro?

Lying Shapiro (lyingshapiro.com) is an independent parody and media-criticism archive that documents Brian Shapiro's debate rhetoric: a 415-debate catchphrase corpus, a polite-dodge tracker, the Wilson Files (his 20 debates with Andrew Wilson), a 63-clip soundboard of real audio, and an index of 429 of his videos. It is not affiliated with Pushing The Limits.

Why is the site called Lying Shapiro?

“Brian, why you always lying?” is the accusation his debate opponents and callers throw at him, and the name of this parody archive. Shapiro insists he has never lied; the archive’s position is that his catchphrases and dodges are documented either way, 5,205 hits and counting.
